# Image credits

Demo images for the fake Ado-Ekiti restaurants. None of them comes from a real
vendor, a delivery app, a restaurant website or the live Behtafood app.

- **19 openly licensed photos** from Wikimedia Commons (no people, no watermarks, no brand logos).
- **12 drawn images** (restaurant wordmark logos and illustrated avatars), drawn by a script for this demo. Not photos of anyone.
- **0 AI-generated images.**

Licence mix: CC BY-SA 2.0: 2, CC BY-SA 3.0: 1, CC BY-SA 4.0: 15, CC0: 1, Same licence as this repository: 12.

Every photo was cropped, resized and re-compressed as JPEG. A photo under a
CC BY-SA licence stays under that same licence after these changes; the
licence links below give the terms. `manifest.json` has the same data in
machine-readable form.
